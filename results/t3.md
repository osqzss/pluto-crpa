## Findings

- **The inter-channel phase is 172.4 to 173.6 deg at the operating point over the whole day (172.4 to 172.8 deg in runs A to D1), and does not change on a retune, a rewrite of the LO or a sample rate change.** Within each run the spread is 0.15 deg (std), at most 0.5 deg. T2 gave 173.0 to 173.4 deg from the PL covariance on 2026-11-09.
- **Amplitude: x2 is 0.3 dB below x1**, wideband and on the carrier (T2: 0.4 dB).
- B1 differs from A, B2 and C by +0.35 deg and -0.05 dB in all 30 captures, and B2, run next, is back at the A value. So a capture 0.5 s after a retune from 2400 MHz is slightly off against the state after a rewrite or a 1 MHz step. Cause not determined (settling of the tracking loops after the far retune is one candidate; not tested with a longer `--settle`).
- Delta: about 1 deg total from +2 to -3 MHz, i.e. over LO 1572.42 to 1579.42 MHz and baseband -4 to +3 MHz.
- Gain: the carrier phase moves from 174.4 deg at 40 dB to 172.6 deg at 65-70 dB (1.8 deg); the carrier ratio stays at -0.3 to -0.5 dB. The wideband ratio falls to -3.2 dB at 40-45 dB because the noise floor of ch0/1 is about 3 dB above ch2/3 at low gain, as seen before (`results/t1_rx_unit_compare.md`); this is noise, not signal gain. No clipping at 70 dB (peak 187 of 2047).
- No source (T3-0): |rho| 0.0066 with a phase near 0 deg in every capture, where independent noise would give about 0.001. A small correlated part exists without the bladeRF (T2-A: 0.006). Cause not determined. Against the common signal at |rho| 0.28 it can shift the wideband phase by about 0.2 deg; the CW phase is not affected, and the two agree within 0.1 deg in A to C.
- **Power cycles reproduce the state: 173.23 to 173.27 deg and -0.30 dB after each of three cycles**, flat over 30 minutes from boot while the chip warms by 4 C. Temperature in the range 29-33 C does not move the phase or the ratio.
- **RX gain writes change the RX1 level at 60 dB.** Each change of gain and return raises x1 by a little, +0.3 dB in total after about four changes, then no further; the carrier phase rises by about 0.4 deg with it. RX2 does not move. A power cycle clears it. This is the step from -0.30 to -0.54 dB between T3-D1 and T3-D2 (the first gain sweep), and the reason the noise-only check after T3-H read RX1 0.3 dB above T3-0. Mechanism in the AD9363 or its driver not identified. Consequence: the `rx` command of `crpa_ctl`, or any gain write after boot, leaves RX1 in a slightly different state; for comparable runs set the gain once after boot.
- **The 173 deg is not a property of the Pluto alone.** Swapping the legs moves the phase from 173.3 to 43.9 deg. The two legs of the fixture differ by 64.7 deg (or -115.3 deg) at L1, and the Pluto's own RX2-RX1 difference is 108.6 deg (or -71.4 deg). 64.7 deg at L1 is 0.114 ns, about 2.3 cm of coax at a velocity factor of 0.66 (-115.3 deg: 0.203 ns, about 4 cm), so unequal cable lengths or the splitter would explain it; not checked. The "channels nearly in antiphase" of T2 was the sum of both parts in that cabling. The amplitude difference is the Pluto's: -0.25 dB, fixture -0.06 dB.
- Connector repeatability (G2): 0.7 deg in phase and under 0.01 dB after unplugging and re-mating both legs twice. So the split of T3-G is good to about 1 deg, apart from the 180 deg ambiguity. The same size of effect, from cables or connectors moving, would cover the two unexplained shifts below (172.4 deg in the first boot, 0.7 deg within T3-H); this is a candidate, not shown.
- **Not explained:** T3-A to T3-D1 read 172.4 deg in a boot whose history before T3-0 is not recorded, 0.8 deg below the three power cycles; and the carrier phase rose 0.7 deg within T3-H (minutes 8 to 17) with no setting changed. Neither is temperature (F1) nor a gain write (none in that time).
- For power inversion these shifts are irrelevant (weights follow R every block). For any fixed calibration (beam steering), they set the scale: about 1 deg and 0.3 dB between states of the same unit on one day.
- After `bladegps` was stopped with `q` (bladeRF still plugged in), 5 captures showed no carrier line and |rho| 0.003 (`t3_A2_20261110-121258.txt`). Earlier the carrier had stayed after `bladegps` exited (`docs/test-equipment.md`, Section 4); not followed up. In these captures x1 read -42.28 dBFS against -42.53 in T3-0 (x2 unchanged at -42.81); AD9363 temperature 29 C against 20-21 C.

## Result

**PASS.** The inter-channel phase and amplitude are recorded per retune, LO rewrite, sample rate change, application dry run (PL) and power cycle. In the T2 cabling: 172.4 to 173.6 deg over the day and -0.3 dB, 173.25 deg +/- 0.03 after each of three power cycles, spread within a run 0.15 deg.
