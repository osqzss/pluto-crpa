# T5: Jammer Power Sweep, J/S Improvement

Plan: `docs/t5_plan.md`. Status: **in progress** (2026-11-18). Done: P0
(tools, `crpa_ctl` write check), P1, P2 at gain 60, P3, and the gain-60
group G60-N, G60-F, G60-P. Next: the gain-50 group (P2 at gain 50 with the
40 dB jammer attenuation, G50-N, G50-F, G50-P).

## Summary so far (gain 60)

- Without suppression the u-blox loses 6 dB of C/N0 at J/N60 13 to 16 dB
  and the fix at 25 to 28 dB. With PI it cold-starts under J/N60 35 dB
  (TTFF 31 s) and keeps C/N0 within +0.3 / -0.5 dB of the fixed-weight
  reference up to the ADC clip onset (J/N60 42.2 dB), and the fix beyond
  it (12 to 13 dB lower C/N0 with the ADC clipping).
- **J/S improvement: 26 to 29 dB at the 6 dB point; more than 14 to 21 dB
  at the fix-loss point** (lower bound: PI did not lose the fix in the
  measurable range).
- Hardware null depth: model values to J/N60 35, then a ceiling near
  75 dB; the residual jammer stays 28 to 35 dB below the noise. First
  degradation before clipping: nonlinearity near ADC full scale (y noise
  +1.7 to +2.9 dB at J/N60 41.9). The sections follow the order of the runs.

Unit: second Pluto (CRPA unit), `crpa_ctl` from commit 8eece67 (write
check). Delta +3 MHz, fs 10 MSPS, bw 9 MHz, INT_LEN 10000, OUT_SHIFT 0,
alpha 0. Cabling V3 as stated by the user: bladeRF (`./bladegps -e
brdc3300.18n`) -> 40 dB -> SPL1 -> CMB1 / CMB2 -> RX1 / RX2; B205mini `TX/RX`
-> 30 + 20 dB -> SPL2 -> jammer legs (50 cm to CMB1 / RX1) -> CMB1 / CMB2;
Pluto TX1 -> DC block -> u-blox. Jammer at L1 + 200 kHz, amplitude 0.5.

**J/N60**: jammer power over the noise power at RX gain 60 dB, per element,
from the x1 level: (P_x1 - P_noise) / P_noise with P_noise -42.55 dBFS
(P2). It is the jammer scale of all T5 runs, also at gain 50.

Findings:

- **Hardware null ceiling of about 75 dB.** Up to J/N60 35 the null is
  within 3.1 dB of the matched model (1.8 dB at 30, as in T4); from 37 dB
  the model keeps growing by about 2 dB per dB and the hardware does not:
  74.7 dB at 39.5, 74.2 dB at 41.9 (model 87). The criterion "within 3 dB of
  the model up to the clip onset" is met to J/N60 35 and missed from 37.2
  dB on. This is the hardware limit T5 is to find; it costs nothing at the
  receiver: the remaining jammer in y is 28 to 35 dB below the noise at
  every unclipped step. Weights are at the ideal (|w2/w1| = 1.022 = 0.19 dB,
  arg = 180 deg + phase of R21, within 0.01 deg), and the scatter of w2
  between blocks is 1e-4, so the ceiling is not the weights; candidates are
  a part of the jammer that differs between the channels (Section 5.3 of the
  plan; e.g. the B205mini sidebands or a difference of the two paths across
  the line width). Not resolved.
- **First limit before clipping: y noise +2.7 to +2.9 dB at J/N60 41.9**,
  with no sample at the ADC limit (PEAK 1985 / 1963 in every block, 0 of
  31 M captured samples clipped). In the same step the 2nd harmonic in y
  grows by 11 dB (to 41 dB over the floor) for 2.4 dB more jammer.
  Inference: nonlinearity of the ADC or the analog chain near full scale,
  different in the two channels, so not nulled. The PI loss at J/N60 41.9 is
  therefore about 3 dB of C/N0 (the carrier gain in y is unchanged).
- **Clipped (+ steps):** null 62 to 64 dB, y noise +8 to +9 dB, the 3rd
  harmonic (-1.583 MHz) and other products in y at 36 to 44 dB over the
  floor. The weights stay near the jammer solution (|w2/w1| 0.999, arg
  87.9 to 88.3 deg). Expected C/N0 loss with PI about 8 dB from the noise
  rise, against 11 dB in the model.
- **PI removes the floor rise next to the jammer**: +1.5 to +4.1 dB in x1,
  +0.2 to +0.8 dB in y. That part is common to the two channels.
- Carrier gain in y over x1 +3.3 dB at every step, clipped steps included
  (Gs predicted +3.48 dB).
- Integrity: 188364 blocks, STATUS 1 in all (no FRAME_ERR), no saturated
  block (OUT_SHIFT 0). Missed blocks 146 to 202 per step: 10 captures per
  step at about 17 per capture, as in T4 (P0).
- **End of the run:** `crpa_ctl` stopped at 190 s of 400 with `ERROR: TX
  data_sel changed to [0 0 0 0]` and the confirmed safe state, about 86 s
  after the jammer quit and the last capture (09:22:12 local). T4 V3-N ended
