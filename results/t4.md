Plan: `docs/t4_plan.md`. Status: **in progress** (2026-11-10). Done: P1 to P3
in V1, P3, the null run N and the fixed-weight run F in V3. V3-P still to do.

`fw_version` v0.39-22-g8f9c-dirty. Delta +3 MHz, fs 10 MSPS, bw 9 MHz, RX gain

the jammer leg to CMB1 (RX1) is a 50 cm cable.
