# T4: Conducted CW Jammer, Power Inversion

Plan: `docs/t4_plan.md`. Status: **in progress** (2026-11-10). Done: P1 to P3
in V1, P3 and the null run N in V3. Receiver runs (F, P) still to do.

Unit: second Pluto (CRPA unit, serial `1044734c96050011f2ff2100408f4f2ac3`),
`fw_version` v0.39-22-g8f9c-dirty. Delta +3 MHz, fs 10 MSPS, bw 9 MHz, RX gain
60 dB manual on both, INT_LEN 10000, OUT_SHIFT 0. No Pluto transmission in
anything below: TX LO powered down and TX attenuation 89.75 / 89.75 dB, read
back at the start and end of every capture set.

Cabling as stated by the user: bladeRF (`./bladegps -e brdc3300.18n`, TX gain
17 dB) -> 40 dB -> SPL1 -> CMB1 / CMB2 -> RX1 / RX2; B205mini `TX/RX` ->
30 + 30 dB -> SPL2 -> two jammer legs -> CMB1 / CMB2; Pluto TX1 -> DC block ->
u-blox. V1: both jammer legs as first cabled (the leg to CMB1 is 25 cm). V3:
the jammer leg to CMB1 (RX1) is a 50 cm cable.

## V3-N: null depth, RX-only (17:13 to 17:16)

`crpa_ctl run --mode pi --load 24 --cap-sel --duration 240 --out /tmp/t4_V3N`
(dry run, by the user); `bladegps` and the jammer on; 10 passive captures of
y (ch0/1) and x1 (ch2/3) per level with `t3_phase.py --passive --cap y
--cw-offset 0,200e3 --cw-span 15e3`. Logs: `t4_V3N_g55/g65/g75_*.txt`,
`t4_V3N_jammer.txt`, `t4_V3N.txt` (crpa_ctl), `t4_V3N_every100.csv`.

- The residual line is above the floor at every level, so these are values,
  not lower bounds. The hardware reaches the model values.
- Criterion "null depth at least J/N + 10 dB": depth minus J/N is 8.8 dB at
  L3, 18.1 dB at L4 and 27.5 dB at L5. L3 misses it by 1.2 dB; the model
  itself gives 18 dB there, so the criterion is tighter than the model at L3.
  Reported to the user; decision on the criterion open.
- bladeRF carrier in y against x1: +3.0 to +3.3 dB (prediction for D = 100
  deg: Gs +3.5 dB). y stays at the noise level plus the noise gain while x1
  goes from -32.1 to -12.9 dBFS.

- arg(w2/w1) = 180 deg + phase of R21 at every level, as PI should give. The
  ideal for the jammer alone at gain 55 dB (P3: -92.29 deg, -0.15 dB) is
  abs 1.018, arg 87.71 deg; L4 and L5 are within 0.6 deg and 0.07 dB of it.
  At L3 the weight is smaller (0.90) because the noise is not negligible.
- At L4 and L5 |w2| would exceed 1; `crpa_ctl` scales w1 down (0.989, 0.975)
  and keeps |w2| at 1.000.
- With `bladegps` on and the jammer still off (4 s), |w2| went to 0.14: PI
  turns to the bladeRF carrier and satellites, as expected.
- Integrity: 176540 blocks, STATUS 1 in all (no FRAME_ERR), no saturated
  block, peak 530 LSB. Missed blocks: 5 at startup and 495 during the 30
  captures (about 16 per capture, as in P0), none otherwise.
- The run ended after 178 s, not 240 s, with `ERROR: TX data_sel changed to
  [0 0 0 0]` and then the confirmed safe state (TX attenuation 89.75 dB, CTRL
  0, TX LO powered down). This is what a `crpa_ctl off` from a second login
  during the run would cause; not confirmed with the user. The captures had
  ended 110 s before.
