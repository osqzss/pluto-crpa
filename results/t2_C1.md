## Interpretation

C/N0 improves by 1.8 dB over T2-A, with all 11 PRNs, and the receiver confirms the 173 deg phase: the in-phase sum gains instead of cancelling. Two effects are mixed, since TX attenuation stayed at 50 dB:

1. **Array gain** on noise that is independent per channel (mostly the Pluto receiver noise): signal +6 dB, that noise +3 dB.
2. **TX output level**: y is 3.7 dB stronger at the TX1 port, so noise added after the combiner (AD9363 TX chain, the path to the u-blox) is relatively smaller. The lower AGC (1872 vs. 2028) shows the receiver sees more power; T1 already found C/N0 sensitive to the TX level.

The control run T2-D (`results/t2_D.md`) separates them: w = [1,0], OUT_SHIFT 1 (y = 2 x1, +6.0 dB) and TX1 attenuation 52.25 dB, the same TX1 output power as T2-C1 (+3.77 dB) without array gain. **T2-D gives the T2-A C/N0 within 0.1 dB, so effect 2 is negligible and the 1.8 dB is array gain** (at most 3 dB for noise independent per channel). An earlier estimate here of +1.2 dB, from a model in which the Pluto only adds independent noise to the reference, was too simple and is withdrawn.

## Result

**PASS**: coherent combining of both channels at RF works, with no saturation or frame errors, and C/N0 improves by 1.8 dB over a single channel, all of it array gain (T2-D).
