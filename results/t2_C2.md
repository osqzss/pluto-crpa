## Expected vs. measured

With x2 0.4 dB below x1 (amplitude ratio 0.955) and 173.4 deg apart, |1 + 0.955 e^(j173.4 deg)| = 0.121: the common signal is 18.3 dB down while the independent noise adds 2.8 dB, about 21 dB worse SNR at the Pluto than a single channel. Measured: 19.8 dB below T2-A in receiver C/N0, consistent within the receiver resolution and the scatter of the few epochs used.

## Result

**PASS** (as a null test): w = [1,1] places a null on the common signal, about 20 dB deep in C/N0, and the receiver loses the fix. Together with T2-C1 (+1.8 dB in phase) this confirms the inter-channel phase seen in R21 at the receiver, and that both weight paths work with the expected sign. T2 is complete.
